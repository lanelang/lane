## Description
Callable conversion has had two identity producers: source-type adaptation
planning and late VM CFG body equivalence. The attempted repair introduced a
persistent Callable Adapter Recipe with its own evidence, layout, nested
adapter, provenance, and occurrence models. That removed late rediscovery but
turned one structural conversion into another shallow compiler subsystem.

## Design
Representation Elaboration owns one operation:

```text
adapt(source_callable_contract, target_callable_contract, value)
```

The operation recursively aligns parameters, evidence, and result contracts.
Identity emits nothing. Direct invocation may fuse conversion into the call.
First-class escape materializes one worker for the complete structural source
and target contracts; each occurrence supplies its own captured values.

The memo table that prevents duplicate workers is private implementation state.
It is not emitted as a recipe catalog or occurrence sidecar. Recursive callable
ABI identities come from the Physical Callable ABI Graph owned by physical ABI
interning, not from adapter-specific graph canonicalization.

VM CFG function-table cleanup owns reachability and general exact duplicate
removal only. It does not recognize adapters or reconstruct their identity.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] One structural adaptation operation owns callable parameter, evidence,
      and result conversion.
- [ ] Identity and directly fused conversions allocate no worker, environment,
      closure, or function-table entry.
- [ ] First-class escape materializes at most one worker for a complete
      structural source/target contract while preserving site-specific captures.
- [ ] The implementation exposes no adapter recipe catalog, nested-recipe
      graph, transition-occurrence sidecar, or source-type identity key.
- [ ] VM CFG canonicalization contains no adapter-specific equivalence logic.
- [ ] Recursive callable ABI interning contains only physical call
      compatibility, not source binders or specialization demand.
- [ ] Black-box tests cover direct fusion and two escaping sites sharing one
      worker; white-box tests are limited to otherwise unobservable recursive
      ABI and one-worker invariants.
- [ ] Explore reports no regression in adapters, erase/unerase operations,
      closures, environments, functions, or Wasm instructions.
- [ ] `moon info`, `moon fmt`, `moon check --target native --warn-list +73`,
      `moon test --target native`, and the valid examples gate pass.
