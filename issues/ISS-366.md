## Description
Three valid examples disagree between LoisVM's interpreter and the default Wasm
JIT path. `21_functional_programming` traps with an out-of-bounds SIGBUS,
`84_f32_operations` silently computes an incorrect assertion result, and
`96_integer_set_adt` reaches an emitted `unreachable`. All three succeed under
`--no-jit`.

The emitted modules pass `wasm-tools validate`, and Wasmtime executes them
without trapping when Lane runtime imports are stubbed. This establishes a JIT
path defect but does not yet prove whether its owner is Wasmoon, MilkIR, or
Lane's translation and optimization contract. The three symptoms must be
minimized independently before claiming a common cause.

## Design
Use one deterministic differential seam that compiles each focused source once
and executes the same bytecode image with the LoisVM interpreter, Wasmoon
interpreter, and Wasmoon JIT. Preserve semantic observations rather than backend
text or incidental function IDs.

The original failures ceased reproducing after the published Wasmoon family and
Lane pipeline advanced, before a minimized failing artifact was retained. Close
the active correctness issue only after the three source shapes and the three
real examples agree on the current published baseline. Do not attribute the
historical fault to a specific owner without evidence, and do not introduce a
JIT disablement, optimization downgrade, runtime fallback, fixture skip, or
source-specific branch.

## Acceptance Criteria
