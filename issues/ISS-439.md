## Description
`compile_object_construction` expands allocation, reference-count/layout
initialization, tags, witnesses, and field stores at each construction site.
The ISS-434 baseline attributes 26,551 of 65,788 Wasm instructions to object
operations, 9,179 to callable operations, 7,308 to CFG structuring, and 5,642 to
ownership. These are expansion categories, not measured removable byte counts.
Inline checks may be counted inside those categories rather than RuntimeGuards.

Earlier `wasm-opt --simplify-locals` and `wasm-opt -Oz` experiments reduced the
164,831-byte baseline to 148,910 and 116,267 bytes respectively. This shows
optimization opportunity, but neither experiment isolates instruction selection
as the cause or proves that all transformations should be reimplemented in Lane.

## Design
First remove demonstrated upstream work. Then compare remaining Physical
operations with emitted Wasm and actual code-body bytes, preserving lineage.
Separate unavoidable object/ownership semantics from repeated emitter protocol,
local-value traffic, and CFG expansion. Retest any external optimizer comparison
with its exact version and commands before using it as current evidence.

Choose a focused backend change only after attributing the residual cost. Do
not precommit to outlining, stackification, size budgets, a second runtime ABI,
or wholesale guard removal. If a new design decision is required, discuss it
with the user; amend scope or create a focused follow-up rather than silently
growing this task. A justified finding that no backend change is warranted is
valid, but must include the measurements and explain remaining costs.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Reproduce the Basic workload after ISS-435 through ISS-438, recording
      revisions, emitted section/body bytes, and owner-produced scale metrics.
- [ ] Attribute the largest remaining expansions to specific operations and
      representative functions, not a cross-IR instruction ratio alone.
- [ ] Document the selected change or evidence-backed decision not to change;
      distinguish guaranteed savings from unproven candidates.
- [ ] Any implemented optimization has behavior and structural regressions,
      including relevant ownership/failure paths, and passes native tests,
      examples, and the real Basic suite.
