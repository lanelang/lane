## Description
The Coroutine Scheduler currently reaches initial VM CFG with 17 direct calls
and 49 indirect calls, so approximately 74 percent of calls remain indirect.
Bytecode retains the same 49 indirect calls. Their current provenance is 18
calls in adapter functions, 16 calls in source-provenance functions, 14 calls
in anonymous generated functions, and one call in an initializer.

Some continuations genuinely escape through `Step` and `Queue`, but the report
cannot distinguish source-semantic escape from temporary CPS flow or
adapter-only indirection. Without that distinction, devirtualization either
misses avoidable calls or risks changing higher-order semantics.

## Design
The typed ANF callable-instance plan owns logical callable flow, and VM CFG
UseDef/callable-flow analysis owns final physical target and environment facts.
Together they must assign every indirect call one structured reason:

- the callable escapes because of source semantics;
- the callable is generated by CPS but remains dynamically selected;
- the indirection exists only at a representation-adapter boundary; or
- the target set is genuinely unknown or contains multiple compatible targets.

Explore consumes this classification; it must not derive it from labels,
function names, spans, storage positions, or rendered IR. Devirtualize only
singleton target sets with an exact environment and callable ABI. Do not add a
fallback opcode, a first-seen target rule, or a call-count heuristic.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Every VM CFG and bytecode indirect call has a structured classification
      and typed provenance produced by the owning analysis.
- [ ] Source-semantic escapes, CPS-generated flow, and adapter-only indirection
      are reported as distinct categories.
- [ ] Singleton target sets with exact environment and callable ABI lower to
      direct calls, including flows through aliases, parameters, results, and
      immutable aggregate fields.
- [ ] Multi-target, open-generic, mutable, and genuinely escaping callables
      remain indirect without speculative guards or fallback dispatch.
- [ ] The Coroutine Scheduler contains fewer than the recorded 49 indirect
      calls, and every remaining indirect call has a reviewed necessity reason.
- [ ] Direct and indirect execution produce the same observable result under
      the interpreter and Wasm JIT.
- [ ] `moon info`, `moon fmt`, `moon check --target native --warn-list +73`, and
      `moon test --target native` pass.
