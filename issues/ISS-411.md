## Description
Known constructors still survive as heap aggregates through Runtime ANF and
Physical Lowering even when every use is a local projection, constructor match,
or reconstruction. The generated `make_data`, field projections, consume
operations and ARC traffic then expand substantially in Wasm.

## Design
Perform scalar replacement at the earliest verified IR that owns complete use
and evaluation-order facts. A single Core rewriter validates and rewrites the
complete lexical use closure: every use must be a destructuring match for the
known constructor, no match may retain the whole value, and no use may cross a
function, type abstraction, or recursive binding boundary. All projections
share field bindings evaluated once at the original construction point.

Do not introduce a second escape-analysis sidecar, specialize nominal storage,
or recover source constructors from Physical instructions. Unsupported use
forms simply retain the aggregate; this is the optimization's proof boundary,
not a representation fallback.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [x] Local known construct/project/match chains avoid aggregate allocation.
- [x] Effectful payloads retain source evaluation order and exactly-once
      execution.
- [x] Escaping, recursive, whole-value, existential and unsupported
      aggregates retain ordinary allocation.
- [x] Removed aggregates also remove their projections and associated ARC work
      before Physical emission.
- [x] Black-box behavior tests cover positive and negative cases; private
      ownership/use invariants use only necessary white-box tests.
- [x] Explore shows a strict structural reduction on at least one pinned real
      program without making the measured value an optimization threshold.

## Close Notes
- 2026-08-28: Core now scalar-replaces local constructors only when one
  validating rewrite consumes every use as a known destructuring projection.
  Effectful fields remain ordered and execute once. The committed source
  regression has zero Physical object shapes; pinned Basic drops one Physical
  function, 21 Physical instructions, 15 slots, two indirect calls, and 295
  Wasm instructions. Scheduler is unchanged, confirming the rewrite is
  structural rather than a corpus-wide threshold policy. Verified with 1714
  native tests, the complete example corpus, and pinned Basic under both JIT
  and interpreter.
